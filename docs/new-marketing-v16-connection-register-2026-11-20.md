No action, advertising transaction, reminder, provider collection or live daily feed change follows a tab/assumption interaction.

## Simulated analytics source proposal

Policy `synthetic-session-events-v1` explicitly generates non-purchasing visit observations from a fixed date-seeded exposure budget (1,200 plus a deterministic 0–900). A source-stage partition allocates those observations to browse-only/cart-only/checkout-abandon stages using disclosed 86:10:4 weights. These are simulation source parameters, not claimed merchant rates or conversion inferred by dividing orders by a desired percentage. Original synthetic orders are explicitly grouped into purchasing sessions; some adjacent orders share a session under a deterministic one-or-two-order rule. The source retains session/order links; the small monthly payload retains daily integer counts and a proof hash. Complete daily coverage and count inequalities are independently checked. Purchasing conversion and checkout completion are derived in the webpage, never persisted ratio cells.

Net revenue segmentation separately uses exact existing sale and refund event metrics. It does not use 39%/43% customer counts, a 98% revenue mix assumption or a fixed 80% acquisition budget. General merchant customer/session definitions and provider acceptance remain separate from this reviewed synthetic-only proposal.
