## Discrepancies that block simply wiring old RPCs

Concrete definitions are in the original catalog's `functions[]`; supporting recovered SQL is under `db-migrations/reconciliation/2026-09-08/recovered/`.

- `contribution_margin_pct` deducts assumed payment/fulfilment/packaging/shipping/return-handling/marketing rates but **no product COGS**. It uses legacy sales and counts; its denominator is net sales without separately established net shipping. Agreed contribution requires historical COGS and uses net product sales plus net shipping for margin denominators. Its comments advising static fallbacks are not current policy.
- `operating_profit_monthly` multiplies that incomplete ratio by legacy net sales and subtracts `monthly_overhead_total`. It is not independently validated operating profit simply because a named RPC exists.
- `monthly_overhead_total` sums fully contained periods, filters `oc.is_active=true`, and `COALESCE`s missing rows to zero. This can omit recurring monthly costs when a week is selected, remove historical inactive categories and hide incomplete coverage. It also needs explicit currency/classification reconciliation.
- `cash_runway_months` divides latest snapshot cash by current-month overhead, not the agreed last-three-complete-month actual burn. Its snapshot aggregation does not classify restrictions/unsettled funds or establish complete same-currency accounts.
- The 8 September original dataset measured 1,463 non-cancelled lines/units without linked variant costs; the known-cost £68,116.50 subtotal was not total COGS. Refund totals on orders exceeded linked event totals. These are historical original observations, not new staging defects or an invitation to backfill guessed numbers.

## What the prototype already demonstrates — not installed inputs

`experiments/financial-v1/source-schema.sql` provides `source_orders.historic_unit_cost,cost_evidence`, `source_refunds.saleable_quantity,returned_date,recovery_evidence`, `source_costs.event_date,currency,amount,category,provenance`, and `source_coverage.costs_complete`. These directly address several missing contracts. Its expense categories are variable/advertising/overhead/depreciation_amortisation, with actual/estimated provenance. The code/tests are reusable evidence for designing an integration, but these disposable table names are **not** a second production dataset to query or silently install.

By contrast E's actual `finance_v1.coverage_evidence` has `sales_and_refunds_complete` only. Its applied order/refund evidence contains no historical COGS, saleable recovery or expense verification columns. A sales-complete flag cannot unlock profit. Cash prototype helpers also depend on caller-provided account/flow completeness and do not ingest a bank feed.

## Minimal coherent first implementation package — proposal

1. Establish a single **member-authorised reporting snapshot** for one selected store, currency and complete calendar month, reusing verified sales/refund evidence. Return per-component availability and provenance; keep valid sales available when costs are unsupported. Reporting pages consume this common contract, not hardcoded sample reporting totals or differing legacy RPC formulas.
2. Prepare a narrowly scoped cost-evidence extension against the **existing staging objects**, reusing overhead/spend structures where suitable. Add explicit immutable cost-at-sale and saleable-return evidence plus classified expense/coverage evidence where the inventory shows a genuine gap. Do not create duplicate accounting values merely to make a screen match. Existing source-adapter tests guide failure cases; cost allocations and unresolved policies remain explicit.
3. For the first staged demonstration, prepare one deterministic synthetic month in that same Supabase dataset, with complete declared test inputs and links for historical cost, return recovery, variable expenses, advertising, overheads and D&A. Keep synthetic provenance unmistakable. This requires a reviewed setup/migration/population package and separate database approval; today's map is not permission to apply it.
4. Reconcile gross → net product sales → COGS → contribution after marketing → operating profit → EBITDA in the shared calculation contract, test missing/stale/cross-store/currency/duplicate evidence and later refunds, then connect Profit Overview/Margin reporting. Scenario assumptions remain separate unsaved modelling over a disclosed baseline; they must not mutate actual reporting.
5. Leave cash/runway unavailable for this first package. They require a distinct account/flow evidence workstream, not arbitrary coefficients or overhead-based burn. After the reporting contract is coherent, use Shopify development to populate the same source/evidence path, rather than another dashboard-specific feed.
