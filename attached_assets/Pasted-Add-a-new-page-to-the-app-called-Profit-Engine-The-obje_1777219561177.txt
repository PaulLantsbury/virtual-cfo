As the sliders move, dynamically calculate and display:

Projected Revenue

Projected Fixed Costs
Projected EBITDA
EBITDA Movement vs Base
Projected EBITDA Margin

Base assumptions:
Revenue = 520000
Contribution margin = 38%
Fixed costs = 120000
Base EBITDA = 78000

Simplified calculation:
Adjusted revenue = revenue * (1 + revenue change)
Adjusted contribution margin = base contribution margin - discount rate change - returns rate change - variable cost change
Projected contribution = adjusted revenue * adjusted contribution margin
Projected fixed costs = fixed costs * (1 + fixed cost change)
Projected EBITDA = projected contribution - projected fixed costs
Projected EBITDA margin = projected EBITDA / adjusted revenue

Show a clear AI-generated interpretation below the results:
- If EBITDA increases: “This scenario strengthens the profit engine because contribution growth more than offsets fixed cost movement.”
- If EBITDA decreases: “This scenario weakens the profit engine because margin pressure or fixed cost growth absorbs too much contribution.”
- If EBITDA becomes negative: “This scenario pushes the business below break-even. Management should prioritise margin recovery or fixed cost control.”
