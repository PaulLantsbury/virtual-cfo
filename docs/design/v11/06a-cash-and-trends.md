## Profit to bank

£35,533 EBITDA − £24,000 inventory increase − £5,000 debtor increase − £3,000 provider balance increase + £12,000 supplier credit increase − £1,533 other operating adjustments = £14,000 operating cash before tax and interest. The £1,533 is a stipulated example requiring itemised production evidence. Do not add D&A back to EBITDA again.

£14,000 operating cash − £8,000 tax − £4,000 equipment − £6,000 loan principal − £10,000 dividends = −£14,000 bank movement. Opening bank £200,000 becomes £186,000 at 30 September. Equipment is investing; principal and dividends are financing. Interest, FX and other movements are zero only in this simplified fixture.
