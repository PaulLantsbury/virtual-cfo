# 4A Period comparison and denominator rules

DT01 P1. Historical reports share one context. Period presets: Yesterday, Last week, Last month, Last 3 months, Last 6 months, Last 12 months, Custom. Yesterday is the previous completed business-local day; Last week is the completed Monday–Sunday week; month presets use completed calendar months. Custom uses explicit inclusive dates. First-use default is Last 3 months.

DT02 P1. Comparison choices: Previous equivalent period, Prior week, Prior month, Prior 3 months, Prior 6 months, Prior 12 months, Same period last year, Custom, None. Previous equivalent period is the immediately preceding equal-duration or equal-calendar-period range. Fixed prior periods end before the selected range begins. Same period last year shifts the dates back a year with an explicit leap-day policy. Show both resolved ranges and prevent accidental overlap.

The final historical mockups consistently use 1 July–30 September 2026 versus 1 July–30 September 2025. The fictional snapshot is SAMPLE-Q3-26, dated 30 September 2026. The values are constructed examples, not a relocated real ledger. Bank balances separately show their as-of date.

DT03 P0. Changing context updates totals, charts, commentary, per-order values and evidence atomically. Unequal ranges display a notice and prioritise margin/per-order comparisons. Missing comparison suppresses comparative verdicts. None removes the dotted series and comparative claims. Non-positive or unreliable revenue suppresses unsupported margin interpretation.

DT04 P1. Chart buckets adapt to range and available evidence. Hourly yesterday only when supported, daily short periods, and weekly or monthly longer periods. The final quarterly examples use three monthly buckets to avoid fabricated weekly series. Label aggregation and actual dates; partial buckets and gaps remain explicit.

## Per-order amounts

UN01 P0. Per unit means per original eligible order here, not per product item. Current historical denominator is 9,980 orders; comparison is 9,600. Divide period flows by their own period’s count using full precision. Combined periods use sums divided by summed counts, not averages of daily ratios. Zero/missing counts yield Per order unavailable.

UN02 P1. Scout shows the subordinate per-order amounts under sales, CM3 and EBITDA. Bank cash remains a dated balance. Profit’s Total / Per order switch updates all scoped monetary cards, Sankey, trends, bridge, tables and evidence while preserving filters and expanded sections. Percentage changes recalculate for the selected mode; margins keep their proper numerator and denominator. AOV remains separately defined.

## Other page contexts

The planner shows a read-only baseline and one-month scenario horizon. Historical report context, where displayed, remains separately labelled and does not drive scenario assumptions. Accepted Actions filters by status/review date. Monitoring shows check time, coverage and schedule; history can have a date filter. Settings shows freshness and effective dates, not a global historical filter. Cohort windows remain explicitly independent of the current reporting period.
