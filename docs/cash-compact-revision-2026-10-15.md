# Cash Control: simpler comparison-led revision

Latest review replaces the two aligned charts with one cash-generated versus Profit (EBITDA) grouped comparison on the same scale; sales no longer masks the cash/profit relationship. Profit Engine period/view controls now support last complete week/month and three/six/twelve months, weekly/monthly, prior year or previous twelve-week/three-month average. Selected totals compare against an equivalent-length run rate, never a multiweek total against one week.

The bridge starts from the same EBITDA as Profit Engine, so depreciation is already excluded and is not added back again. Original accrual operating-profit reconciliation remains intact internally. Plain-English, sign-specific categories explain cash tied up in stock, customer money still unpaid, pending payouts and unpaid supplier bills. Supporting category explanations are accessible without hover.

Four sections mirror Profit Engine: summary/CFO, focused chart and bridge, prioritised review actions with standard reminder, then expandable supporting detail/outlook. Daily outlook, optional commitments, warnings, buffer, timing, safeguards and methodology are retained with a compact visible outlook/gaps summary.

All figures remain synthetic. Weekly cash timing is allocated from illustrative monthly movements and does not certify observed bank activity; weekly profit is explicitly estimated. Live member API cash remains unavailable and forecasts remain unvalidated. No new source collection, live schema changes or writer activation.

Validation: independent twenty-seven cash model/outlook/reminder checks pass, including exact Profit Engine EBITDA/period matching, average denominators, monthly-to-weekly cash conservation, calendar comparisons, no second D&A add-back and existing safety behaviour.
