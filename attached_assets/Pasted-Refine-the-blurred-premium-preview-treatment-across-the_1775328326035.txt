Refine the blurred premium-preview treatment across the app so the free version creates strong intrigue but gives away slightly less decision detail.

Apply this globally to all PremiumBlurPreview / PRO-gated sections, especially:

- Opportunities sections
- driver breakdown sections
- channel analysis sections
- premium diagnostic tables/charts

The goal is to preserve:
- curiosity
- trust
- product quality

while reducing how much actionable detail a free user can extract.


PART 1 — Soften action labels in free previews

Where gated opportunity rows currently show fully explicit action names such as:

- Reallocate Meta spend
- Reduce shipping costs
- Lower discount depth

Update the FREE preview version only to show slightly more general action labels such as:

- Reallocate paid spend
- Optimise fulfilment costs
- Improve pricing strategy

Use the more precise wording only for Pro users.

Important:
- do not change the headline opportunity amount visible in free
- do not remove the fact that there are 3 opportunities
- only make the row titles slightly less specific in free mode


PART 2 — Reduce precision of confidence indicators in free previews

Where premium-gated rows currently show:
- High confidence
- Medium confidence
- Low effort
- Medium effort

Update FREE preview versions only to show less precise visual indicators, for example:

Confidence: ●●●○○
Effort: ●●○○○

or a similar masked visual rating system.

Do not show exact text labels like "High confidence" or "Medium effort" in free mode.

For Pro users, keep the full exact labels unchanged.


PART 3 — Increase masking on value columns only

In blurred premium-preview sections, make the right-hand value columns harder to infer than the left-hand category labels.

Apply stronger blur / opacity / masking specifically to:

- £ impact columns
- pp uplift columns
- value labels at end of bars
- summary value rows inside gated previews

Keep left-hand row/category labels more readable than right-hand values.

Goal:
Users can understand what kind of action is hidden,
but cannot clearly infer how valuable it is.


PART 4 — Slightly soften ranking clarity in free previews

Where opportunity rows show:
1
2
3

Reduce the prominence of these ranking numbers in FREE previews by slightly lowering contrast or opacity.

Keep them visible enough to suggest ranking exists,
but avoid making the priority order feel fully explicit.

Do not remove them entirely.


PART 5 — Preserve what should remain visible in free mode

Do NOT change the following:
- headline opportunity value (e.g. £20,400)
- overall number of opportunities shown
- upgrade CTA position
- overall section structure
- ghost value placeholders (e.g. £ —,— and +—.—pp)

The free version should still clearly communicate:
- there is a real quantified opportunity
- there are multiple identified actions
- more precise guidance is available in Pro


PART 6 — Consistency across all pages

Apply these rules consistently anywhere premium blur previews are used across:
- Profit Margin Analysis
- Marketing Efficiency
- Growth Drivers / Growth Quality
- any other PRO-gated analysis pages

Ensure free previews now communicate:
"valuable insight exists here"
without allowing users to extract too much actionable precision.


PART 7 — Final quality check

After implementing:
- free mode should still feel generous and premium
- Pro mode should still feel meaningfully more useful
- blurred sections should create intrigue, not frustration
- no gated preview should look broken, empty, or disabled

Do not redesign the pages — just tighten the premium-preview behaviour.
