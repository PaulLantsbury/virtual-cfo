# 3 Final navigation and page structure

| Main destination | Subviews | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scout’s View | Default, expanded, verified quiet, incomplete evidence | Homepage summary of the same supported specialist results. |
| Profit Engine | Total / Per order; independent diagnostic sections | Explain profit, contribution and fixed-cost coverage. |
| Growth & Marketing | Investment Scorecard; Customer Economics; Channels | Assess acquisition economics and investigate marketing investment. |
| Cash Control | Independent historical diagnostics | Explain profit conversion and bank movements. |
| Plan | Opportunities; Scenario planner; Accepted actions | Choose, model and follow through on a supported action. |
| Monitoring | Setup, current status, history and preferences | Check material changes and review dates when the service exists. |
| Settings | Connections; Business guardrails; Team & access | Maintain trustworthy data, business context and access. |

Use these names consistently in navigation and cross-page actions. Do not recreate retired pricing, discount or margin pages as hidden tabs. Relevant diagnostics live within Profit Engine. Authorised financial review is an exception workflow under Settings. Explore the Numbers is contextual evidence, not a separate main destination.

Preserve bookmarked legacy routes through explicit redirects or tab selection. Earlier inspected aliases included /margin-analysis, /pricing-optimisation and /new-profit to Profit, plus /opportunities and /cfo-alerts. Validate redirects in the implementation. /dashboard/transactions was not an evidence route in the inspected source; /verified-sales was development-only. Do not advertise either as an owner workflow without support.

## Cross-page action destinations

| Action | Destination and preserved context |
| --- | --- |
| Review stock movements | Cash Control stock section; selected period, snapshot, inventory and product evidence. |
| Review discount test | Plan test-assumption review; explicit AOV/order assumptions and guardrails, never an unrelated neutral scenario. |
| Review first-order acquisition | Investment Scorecard acquisition detail with selected customer and spend scope. |
| Review Meta evidence | Channels evidence for Meta, selected metric, attribution and date range. |
| Review cash and guardrails | Checklist of commitments, reserve preference and payback tolerance with owner/adviser handoff. |
| Review assumptions | Planner assumption review for the active method and baseline; saving only after persistence exists. |
