# 13 Earlier observed evidence

The following is historical evidence retained from the earlier review. It describes the inspected implementation, not the final target designs. No fresh live-site or roadmap inspection was performed for version 11. The narrow initial viewport was approximately 453 × 739; later desktop visual captures informed the dark styling. Full desktop/mobile functionality, source reconciliation, persistence and scheduler execution were not certified.

## Profit and Cash replacement demos

Profit showed sales up 1.8% and EBITDA down 53.3%, five headline metrics, bridges and several controlled-test actions. The useful verdict appeared below controls/notices in the narrow viewport. Evidence was distributed without the consistent Explore the Numbers label; saving was browser-local. Conditional annual benefit examples needed their assumptions kept prominent.

Cash showed £186,000 dated bank cash, a £50,000 buffer and historical cover, with an explicit no-live-forecast limitation. It separated profit, operating cash and bank movements. £8,581 stock absorption was historical rather than a recoverable saving. A reminder link exceeded the stated reminder capability. The final design replaces that resilience layout with the historical diagnostics specified above.

## Other staging pages

CFO Briefing, Growth Quality, Marketing Efficiency and Opportunity Finder disclosed unavailable actual analysis and labelled fixed examples. Repeated notices and long sample sections weakened hierarchy. Growth composition mixed potentially overlapping categories. Marketing examples used unvalidated reallocation assumptions; opportunity totals did not resolve overlapping actions. These were not verified recommendation engines.

Scenario Planner exposed Growth, Costs and Overheads sliders with one-month fixed assumptions and unavailable saving/comparison. A later desktop inspection on 30 October confirmed that visual structure, not live data or the newly designed acquisition/conversion modes. Monitoring disclosed fictional checks and notifications. Settings separated collection from financial verification but put technical material in the owner journey.

Financial Review was a limited authorised entry state; deeper behaviour was unverified. The staging pricing page displayed Free and £49/month Pro, while some forecasting/briefing promises and active-monitoring language conflicted with product availability. Public marketing pages were not otherwise visually assessed; their mockups remain outside this scope.

## How to use this evidence

Treat the final requirements as developer changes to implement and validate, not as claims that those changes have already shipped. The roadmap’s later navigation decisions take precedence over older website names. Source-code route behaviour is distinct from observed UI behaviour. The original HoloGrowth scorecard and competitor Margin Calculator screenshots were not supplied, so their exact formulas or appearance are not claimed here.
