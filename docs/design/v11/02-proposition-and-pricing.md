# 2 Customer proposition and commercial approach

## Proposition and ideal customer

Night Scout helps established, growing Shopify businesses turn sales, accounting and marketing data into better financial decisions. It highlights the few issues that matter to profit and cash, explains the evidence and helps owners choose and follow through on practical actions.

Suggested headline: Know what matters to your profit and cash. Decide what to do next. Supporting copy: Connect Shopify and Xero, with Meta and Google Ads where relevant. See what deserves attention and explore the numbers before you act.

The primary customer is an established, growing e-commerce business with £250,000–£5 million annual revenue, a proven product range, Shopify and Xero. The founder or managing director is the main user; finance, operations and advisers need shared evidence. Key jobs are explaining profit pressure, assessing acquisition economics, investigating cash absorption, testing assumptions and reviewing agreed actions.

Differentiation is business-specific financial guardrails, defensible customer economics, concise prioritisation and follow-through. A worsening ROAS alone does not justify cutting spend, and attractive mature cohorts do not establish near-term affordability. These are positioning choices, not verified claims about every competitor.

## Pricing and freemium

| Tier | Proposed experience | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Useful dated snapshot, one supported conclusion, full evidence and data health. | Publish refresh cadence; do not imply continuous monitoring. |
| Core | Richer decision support, scenario and action persistence, monitoring as delivered. | £49/month was observed on staging as Pro; validate price and costs before launch. |
| Team later | Roles, collaborators and adviser workflows when supported. | No additional price or delivery date is agreed. |

Never paywall evidence needed to trust a displayed conclusion, data limitations or a material alert. Billing entitlement, implemented functionality and data readiness are separate gates. Paying does not resolve missing evidence.

A 14-day paid-capability trial from the first usable snapshot is a proposal, not a recovered agreement. Final tariff, VAT presentation, refresh limits, allowances, trial terms, cancellation, retention and export remain commercial decisions. The earlier £99 Team idea is not an agreed price.

Measure first useful answer, evidence use, accepted actions, completed reviews, return usage, conversion and cancellation reasons. Correct any public promises of unsupported forecasts or active briefings before selling them. Preserve permitted dated read access on downgrade under the published retention policy.
