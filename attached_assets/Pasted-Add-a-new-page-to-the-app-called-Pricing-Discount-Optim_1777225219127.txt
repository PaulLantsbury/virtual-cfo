Add a new page to the app called “Pricing & Discount Optimisation”.

This page should mirror the structure, tone, gating and visual style of the existing Profit Engine and Cash Control pages.

The objective is to help ecommerce founders understand:
1. whether discounts are helping growth or damaging margin
2. where contribution is being lost
3. how much profit could be recovered through better pricing
4. what pricing action would improve margin fastest
5. how changes in discounting, full-price orders and returns affect contribution

This page should feel like an AI CFO explaining pricing power in plain English, not a pricing spreadsheet.

Pricing Optimisation
