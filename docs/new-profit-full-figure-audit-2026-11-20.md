## What “linked” means

A calculation can run in the website and still be fully database-backed. The connection belongs to its inputs and their business/period/version scope; it does not require every percentage or bridge bar to be stored in Supabase.

- **Database/API inputs:** selected sales primitives, original-order count, cost components and reviewed snapshot/version evidence arrive through the existing authenticated history-reporting API. They are not read through a browser table grant. The server reads stored mapped transaction/journal evidence and seals, recomputes canonical totals read-only, and returns supported components. The browser then checks/recomputes its own view. Exact storage/aggregation is covered by the coordinator’s backend trace; an API field is not proof that the final displayed ratio was stored.
- **Website calculation:** the browser validates/composes this evidence, recomputes/reconciles financial subtotals, calculates per-order amounts, ratios, comparisons, Sankey values and change-bridge effects. These are linked results when their validated inputs are linked.
- **User/scenario assumption:** a hypothetical rate, review date or cost reduction is not an observed database fact. It should remain explicitly labelled, even if its baseline later becomes database-backed.
- **Static example:** the yellow sample page uses its own frozen `DAILY_SAMPLE` array. It is a different constructed design dataset from the database-backed UK Gifts history. Its correct arithmetic does **not** make its inputs linked.

The connected entry point is `pages/new-profit-connected.tsx` → `components/NewProfitLive.tsx` → `lib/useReviewedHistory.ts` → `/api/history-reporting/months` and `/api/history-reporting`. The shared strict composition is `lib/v11/history-supported.ts`, `history-composition.ts`, `supported-slice.ts` and `calculations.ts`. The browser enforces explicit business/month/version selection, suppresses old/cross-scope/loading evidence and does not fall back to sample figures. The comparison is an explicitly chosen **earlier complete month**, with its own denominator, not an automatic prior-year or rolling period.

## Count and scope

**79 logical numeric output families:** 70 financial/assumption families and 9 numeric context/control families. Every row has a matching record in `review/profit-full-figure-audit.json`.

A count unit is a **logical output/formula family**, not every repeated numeral on the screen. Current/comparison and Total/Per-order are explicitly covered within each family; the same metric repeated in a card, chart label, tooltip and table is not inflated into four metrics. Each core financial metric, each separate bridge driver and each distinct formula has its own row below. Numeric context/controls are listed separately. Axis ticks, currency rounding, section numbers and SVG pixel coordinates are display mechanics, not extra business metrics.

**C** = connected monthly default. **S** = explicit yellow sample design. A C-linked metric does not imply its similarly named S chart has been wired.

## Coverage of expanded screens and repeated displays

- **Headline / five cards:** sales, gross profit, contribution, overheads excluding D&A and EBITDA use the P families. S adds their absolute changes, growth percentages, proper margin/retention ratios, percentage-point changes, subordinate per-order/total values and the operating-profit/D&A reconciliation. C supports the linked amounts and explicit-month comparison, with EBITDA margin/change in its verdict.
- **Main Sales & Profit chart (S):** net product sales bars, EBITDA bars and the selected comparator EBITDA line; tooltip and expanded trend table repeat those values. It always shows the last 12 complete periods independent of headline horizon. The line is either a fixed earlier rolling-reference average or matching prior-year period, not a future forecast. Profit-only hides the sales bars without changing the data.
- **Sankey (C and S):** source net product sales and net shipping; uses product costs, variable costs, advertising, overheads excluding D&A and EBITDA. Values repeat the P/N families. Flow thickness is each amount/revenue and is visual encoding, not another cash metric. Negative/loss cases are refused by the nonnegative Sankey and remain inspectable in signed tables/bridge; no fake positive flows.
- **Change bridge / accessible figures (C and S):** comparison/current EBITDA bookends; eight signed driver effects; a net-sales checkpoint; cumulative levels and connectors. Per-order mode uses each period's own original orders. The checkpoint is not added again. The amount shown above a bar/table and its rectangle represent the same logical value.
- **Graph analysis (S):** EBITDA delta and margin-point change repeat D/PP families; largest positive/negative amounts are selected from the eight existing signed bridge effects. Those maxima describe arithmetic location, not causal explanations or extra adjustments.
- **CFO action cards and expansions (S):** eight possible investigations feed at most three ranked cards. Each repeats the current/reference total and own-denominator per-order values for its metric, adverse signed bridge effect and illustrative annual range only for discount/cost actions. “Confidence”, effort, guardrails and cause claims remain qualitative caveats; other action benefits are intentionally unknown.
- **Combined benefit expansion (S):** repeats the discount-plus-cost annual low/high range and 52/12 annualisation convention. Payment processing is already inside variable costs; it is not added separately. Month 1/2/3 cash-timing statements are qualitative illustrative sequencing, not cash amounts, cash forecasts, dated commitments or guaranteed receipt timings.
- **Biggest changes per order (S):** top three absolute per-order deltas among eligible cost rows excluding D&A; current/reference values repeat P/D families. Its per-order ranking is different from total-pound action ranking.
- **Full breakdown (C and S):** all 14 P rows; S also displays rolling-reference equivalents and prior-year values, 14 D-row variances, direction arrows and relative-width bars. Lower costs are favourable; higher sales/profit are favourable. Current, rolling and prior-year original-order denominators stay separate.
- **Discount/refund charts and expanded tables (S):** each period's value/gross-product-sales percentage, prior-year equivalent, selected-period percentage-point difference and selected-period pound change. These charts are independent of headline horizon and always compare with prior year; Total/Per-order never changes a percentage. Refunds follow refund-event dates and can relate to older orders.
- **Saved plan (S):** owner/review date/status plus a frozen sample metric/order baseline, sample order count/dates and a conditional later sample observation. These are localStorage records scoped by signed-in user/business, not Supabase records or delivered reminders. “Done” is a user flag, not proof of savings. No target benefit is invented.
- **Assumptions / Explore the Numbers:** source dates, model coverage, revision/snapshot IDs and observation times are provenance metadata, not additional profit outputs. C provenance is server-checked evidence; S dates/coverage describe fixed examples. URL review parameters select sample horizons; they do not import the New Plan's sample as a verified business baseline.

3. **Action sensitivities:** bind eligible real sales/orders/cost pools, retain rate reductions/demand response as explicit assumptions, include implementation costs and evidence scope. A database-backed baseline does not validate an assumed achievable saving or prove causality.
4. **Detailed diagnosis:** product mix, promotion tests, refunds/reasons, historical product costs, invoice/service mix and contracted rates are needed for cause/eligibility claims. Aggregate monthly totals alone cannot supply them.
5. **Saved plans:** persist a versioned verified baseline and user-owned action/review history through a separately authorised scoped write path if cross-device operation is wanted. Reminder delivery and realised-benefit measurement need their own evidence. Current local dates/statuses are valid user inputs, not broken financial calculations.
