# Night Scout final product specification and app designs

30 October 2026  |  Version 11  |  Product and developer reference

Night Scout helps established e-commerce owners understand what matters to profit and cash, inspect the evidence and choose a practical next step. This consolidated edition applies the approved owner-review changes to the mockups and replaces the earlier overlapping design notes. Use this edition for design handoff.

The design package is finalised for the agreed scope. It includes 33 full-page visual references, settled page behaviour, financial definitions, implementation priorities and acceptance criteria. The images contain fictional data. They are design references, not evidence that the live product has these capabilities. User testing and production verification remain delivery requirements.

## Mandatory principles for every page

| Principle | Required behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| 10 second rule | The opening view makes the main issue, significance and next step discoverable. |
| Less is more | One dominant conclusion and primary action. Detail expands independently. |
| Intelligent silence | Suppress immaterial findings only after a completed, scoped check. Missing evidence is never an all-clear. |
| Transparency | Explore the Numbers remains available on every page and state, including Free. |
| Visual first | Use a purposeful chart or working panel where helpful. Preserve the Sankey and cash bridge. |
| Forward looking | Support the next decision. Distinguish historical evidence from conditional scenarios and forecasts. |
| Practical action | Open a relevant task with evidence, scope, ownership and review criteria. |

## What changed in this edition

Scout’s View now uses the specialist pages’ cash and marketing story. Actions sit near the main verdict. Customer Economics and Channels have calm entry views. The planner shows an explicit baseline, alternative Growth methods and an assumption-review step. Accepted Actions, Monitoring and Settings have their missing states. Old buffer, runway and pending-design language has been removed from the target specifications.
