## Connected unavailable copy

**Actual cash reporting is incomplete.** Bank account coverage, operating cash movements and payment timing have not yet been reconciled. Connecting Shopify and Xero does not by itself make these cash calculations available. Missing amounts are not treated as zero.

**Future stock commitments not reviewed.** The outlook may omit significant future payments. Confirm there are none, or add an amount, expected date and description; this does not create or pay a bill.

**Illustrative outlook — not a validated forecast.** This demonstration uses fictional balances, payment dates and estimates. It explains the calculation and shows the effect of assumptions; it does not establish accuracy for a connected business.

- `experiments/shopify/queries.mjs`: order/refund transactions, no payouts query.
- `docs/agreed-financial-definitions.md`: operating profit includes depreciation/amortisation; EBITDA is separate; cash balances and cash generation are separate.

Accounting bridge implementation should use operating profit including depreciation/amortisation, add non-cash adjustments once, apply changes in operating assets/liabilities with consistent signs and cut-offs, then separately reconcile tax/investing/financing/other cash movements to bank change. Any VAT balances/payments and interest treatment must be labelled consistently; no bridge residual may silently become a cause.
