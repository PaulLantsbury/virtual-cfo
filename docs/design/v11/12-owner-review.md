## Assessment against the seven principles

The revised opening views have a clear issue and next step; specialist detail is progressively disclosed. The Sankey, cash bridge, payback curve and selected channel trend provide purposeful visuals. Every page has an evidence entry. Historical investigations and assumption reviews support future action without inventing a forecast. Quiet and missing-data examples now show the intended distinction.

This is a design review from an e-commerce owner’s perspective, not a timed test with actual owners. Ten-second comprehension, intelligent suppression, keyboard/mobile behaviour, source accuracy and persistence cannot be certified by screenshots. Those remain measurable implementation gates below, not unresolved design recommendations.

Commercial tariff, individual business guardrail values, source-specific mappings and integration availability still require their normal decisions. They do not reopen the settled navigation and page design. Future richer models remain outside the first version; do not reintroduce retired pages or add extra default metrics.
