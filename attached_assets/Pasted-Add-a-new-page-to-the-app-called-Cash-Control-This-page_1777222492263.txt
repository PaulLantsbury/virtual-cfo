Add a new page to the app called “Cash Control”.

Use simple client-side calculations for now.

Example logic:
- revenue increase improves projected cash modestly
- inventory days increase worsens working capital drag
- supplier days decrease worsens cash
- fixed cost increase reduces runway
- marketing spend increase reduces projected cash

Show dynamic interpretation:
If projected runway improves:
“This scenario improves cash headroom because cash is released back into the business.”

If projected runway weakens:
“This scenario reduces cash runway because working capital and fixed costs absorb cash faster than trading generates it.”

If projected runway is below 2 months:
“This scenario creates a cash risk within 60 days. Slow spend, reduce stock build or renegotiate supplier terms.”

13. Free vs Pro Gating

Use the same gating approach as Profit Engine.

Free version should show:
- Cash Control Summary
- Cash Risk Level
- Cash Safety Buffer
- Cash Trend
- KPI strip
- basic Where Your Cash Gets Trapped bridge
- headline Working Capital Health cards
- locked teaser for detailed driver breakdown
- locked teaser for sensitivity ranking
- locked sensitivity simulator
- locked CFO recommendations

Pro version should show:
- full What Changed Cash This Month driver table and chart
- full bridge chart and table
- full Working Capital Health section
- full Cash Cost Pressure section
- full Cash Sensitivity Simulator
- full CFO Recommendations
- full detailed explanations

Principle:
Free = diagnosis
Pro = action, explanation and modelling
