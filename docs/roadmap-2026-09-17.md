Each merchant needs an explicit, versioned mapping from their own Xero chart of accounts to Night Scout’s accounting categories. The setup flow must present a reviewable matrix rather than assuming that one company’s chart of accounts applies to another:

| Night Scout category | User choice | Suggestion behaviour | Validation before use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Booked revenue | One or more Xero accounts | Suggest from account type, name and prior accepted mappings | User confirms; active, non-overlapping accounts only |
| Processing fees | One or more Xero accounts | Suggest from names such as bank fees, merchant fees or processing fees | User confirms; do not infer a missing account as zero |
| Advertising | One or more Xero accounts | Suggest from advertising/marketing account names | User confirms; duplicate platform/accounting cost treatment remains separately governed |

| Included cash | One or more bank/payment accounts | Suggest active bank/payment accounts; show exclusions separately | User confirms completeness, restrictions and treatment of unsettled funds |

Suggestions are ranked proposals, with the reason and confidence shown, and never activate a mapping or financial result by themselves.
