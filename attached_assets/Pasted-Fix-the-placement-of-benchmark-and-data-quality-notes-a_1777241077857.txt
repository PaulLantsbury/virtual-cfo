Fix the placement of benchmark and data quality notes across the app.

Problem:
Benchmark strips and data quality notes are currently appearing at the top of multiple pages, before the main CFO insight and opportunity sections. This weakens the page hierarchy.

Objective:
Benchmarks and data quality notes should support trust, not lead the page. Move them lower down and make them lower visual priority.

Apply this across all pages:
- Dashboard / Business Control Centre
- Profit Margin Analysis
- Growth Quality
- Marketing Efficiency
- Pricing Optimisation
- Profit Engine
- Cash Control
- Profit Opportunities
- Scenario Lab

1. Remove benchmark/data-quality notes from the top of each page

On every page, ensure the first visible content after the page title is the main CFO verdict, CFO insight, risk summary, opportunity block or page-specific hero section.

Do not show benchmark strips or data quality notes above these sections.

2. Create a reusable collapsible section

Create a reusable component called:

Data & benchmark assumptions

This should contain:
- benchmark note for that page
- data quality note for that page
- confidence explanation where relevant

Default state:
Collapsed

Visual style:
- small
- neutral grey
- subtle border
- low visual weight
- no warning colours unless genuinely critical
- positioned near the bottom of each page

3. Page-specific content

Dashboard:
Benchmark note:
“Dashboard insights combine sales, marketing, pricing, margin and cash data.”

Data quality note:
“Accuracy improves as source data mappings improve.”

Profit Margin Analysis:
Benchmark note:
“Typical healthy DTC contribution margin range: 45–60%. Current: 42.3%.”

Data quality note:
“Margin analysis depends on product costs, shipping, discounts and marketing spend being mapped correctly.”

Growth Quality:
Benchmark note:
“Repeat purchase rate is 28%, within the typical healthy range for this stage.”

Data quality note:
“Growth quality depends on accurate customer, order and discount tagging.”

Marketing Efficiency:
Benchmark note:
“Meta CAC payback is 2.1 orders, above the safe range of <1.2 orders.”

Data quality note:
“Channel contribution estimates rely on revenue attribution from Google and Meta. Cross-channel attribution differences may affect comparisons.”

Pricing Optimisation:
Benchmark note:
“Discount dependency is 38%, above the preferred range of 15–25%.”

Data quality note:
“Discount analysis assumes discounts are recorded using Shopify discount codes or compare-at pricing. Manual price changes may understate discount impact.”

Profit Engine:
Benchmark note:
“Profit health is assessed using contribution margin, overhead load and break-even distance.”

Data quality note:
“Profit analysis depends on accurate fixed cost, contribution and overhead mapping.”

Cash Control:
Benchmark note:
“Cash runway is 3.4 months, within monitor range but below strong cover.”

Data quality note:
“Cash insights depend on accurate stock, supplier payment and bank transaction data.”

Profit Opportunities:
Benchmark note:
“Opportunities are ranked by estimated value, confidence, timing and effort.”

Data quality note:
“Opportunity values are directional estimates based on current connected data quality.”

Scenario Lab:
Benchmark note:
“Scenario quality is assessed against margin, cash runway and CAC payback benchmarks.”

Data quality note:
“Scenario outputs are directional estimates, not guaranteed outcomes.”

4. Keep confidence badges near the relevant insight

Do not move confidence badges away from opportunity cards where they help decision-making.

But any long confidence explanations should move into the collapsible Data & benchmark assumptions section.

5. Preserve page hierarchy

The top of each page should now start with:

Dashboard:
Business Health verdict

Profit Margin Analysis:
Profit Margin Summary / CFO insight

Growth Quality:
Growth Quality verdict

Marketing Efficiency:
Marketing Efficiency Summary / CFO insight

Pricing Optimisation:
Pricing Power summary

Profit Engine:
Profit Engine Summary

Cash Control:
Cash Control Summary / Cash Risk

Profit Opportunities:
Recoverable Opportunity summary

Scenario Lab:
Recommended Scenario / CFO insight

6. Final checks

Ensure:
- no benchmark strip appears as the first card on any page
- no data quality note appears above the main CFO insight
- all notes remain available, just lower priority
- Free/Pro gating remains unchanged
- layout stays clean and responsive
