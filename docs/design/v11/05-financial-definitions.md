# 5 Financial definitions

| Measure | Final definition |
| --- | --- |
| Net product sales | Gross product sales excluding VAT and shipping, less discounts and event-period refunds. Never deduct discounts twice. |
| Average order value | Post-discount original-order product value before later refunds, excluding VAT/shipping, divided by eligible original orders. |
| Gross profit and margin | Net product sales less effective-dated COGS, including only evidenced returned-stock recovery. Gross margin = gross profit / net product sales. |
| Contribution before marketing | Gross profit plus net shipping revenue less applicable fulfilment, payment, delivery and other agreed variable costs. |
| CM3 | Contribution before marketing less advertising once. Agency fees, salaries and software are overheads under the agreed mapping. |
| EBITDA | CM3 less overheads excluding depreciation and amortisation. Operating profit additionally includes D&A. Neither is statutory profit after tax. |
| EBITDA margin | EBITDA / revenue including net shipping, excluding VAT. Do not substitute the gross-margin denominator. |
| Cash | Dated unrestricted settled bank balances. Unsettled provider amounts and reserves remain separate; exclude internal transfers from net movement. |
| New customer CAC | Eligible acquisition spend / identifiable acquired new customers in matched scope. Explain allocation and identity coverage. |
| 90-day GP:CAC | Observed mature cohort gross profit / cohort acquisition cost, equivalently GP per customer / CAC. Not predicted lifetime value. |
| Contribution payback | Earliest observed cohort age when cumulative contribution before acquisition covers CAC. State treatment of repeat marketing; overheads excluded in this fixture. |
| New and returning revenue | Order-level first eligible purchase versus subsequent purchases under the declared identity and refund rules. Unknown identity remains explicit. |

Shopify operational revenue and Xero accounting amounts are not interchangeable. Explain timing, tax, currency and mapping differences. Missing COGS blocks complete profit advice while preserving supported revenue. Test orders are not merchant sales. Record a residual if a reconciliation is incomplete rather than inventing a cause.

## Financial guardrails

Business-specific contribution floors, first-order loss tolerance, observed payback tolerance, reserve preference and owner goals are effective-dated and versioned. No universal 2:1 ratio or arbitrary reserve becomes a rule. “Needs evidence” is an availability state before Scale, Hold, Fix or Reduce. Stop is a bounded stronger action for a named test/campaign, never inferred from one aggregate ratio.
