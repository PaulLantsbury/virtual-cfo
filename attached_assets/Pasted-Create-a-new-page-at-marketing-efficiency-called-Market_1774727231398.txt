Create a new page at /marketing-efficiency called:

Marketing Efficiency

This page should help founders understand whether their marketing spend is generating profitable growth and where budget should be reallocated to improve contribution margin.

The page should feel like a Virtual CFO insight tool rather than a traditional marketing dashboard.

Page structure:

--------------------------------------------------

1. Page title and subtitle

Title:
Marketing Efficiency

Subtitle:
Understand which acquisition channels create profitable customers and where budget should be reallocated.

--------------------------------------------------

Marketing Contribution Margin
Example:
38.6%

Each KPI card should include:
- current value
- change vs previous period
- short explanation text

--------------------------------------------------

3. CFO Insight section near top of page

Add a highlighted insight card titled:

CFO Insight

Example content:

Marketing efficiency has weakened this month due to rising Meta CAC and increased reliance on discounted acquisition.

Blended CAC increased by £3.40 per order, reducing contribution margin across paid channels.

If spend is partially reallocated toward higher-margin channels such as Email and Organic, contribution profit could improve by approximately £14,000–£32,000 next month at current sales volume.

Recommended focus:

• Reduce spend on lowest-margin paid channels
• Increase lifecycle email conversion
• Prioritise acquisition of repeat-purchase customers

Design requirements:

- match styling used for dashboard insight cards
- highlight estimated financial upside
- structure values so they can later be dynamically calculated
