Create a new page at /growth-quality called:

Growth Quality Analysis

This page should help founders understand whether their business is growing in a healthy, profitable way or relying too heavily on discounting and inefficient paid acquisition.

Page structure:

1. Page title and subtitle

Title:
Growth Quality Analysis

Subtitle:
Understand whether growth is being driven by retention, healthy channel mix, and profitable customer acquisition.

2. Top summary KPI cards

Add 4 KPI cards:

- Growth Quality Score
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Discount Dependency
- CAC Payback Period

Example values:
Growth Quality Score: B-
Repeat Purchase Rate: 27%
Discount Dependency: 38%
CAC Payback Period: 1.4 orders

Each card should include:
- current value
- change vs prior period
- short explanation text

3. CFO Insight section near the top of the page

Add a highlighted insight card titled:

CFO Insight

Example content:

Growth quality has weakened this month. Revenue remains positive, but more of that growth is being driven by discounting and higher-cost paid channels rather than repeat customer behaviour.

If discount dependency and CAC efficiency return to prior levels, growth quality could improve materially next month, with an estimated contribution uplift of £12,000–£28,000 at current sales volume.

Include a short list of recommended focus actions:
- Reduce blanket discounting
- Improve retention conversion
- Reallocate spend toward higher-margin channels

Design requirements:
- use the same visual style as the dashboard and margin analysis insight cards
- highlight the estimated financial upside
- structure content so it can later be dynamically generated

4. Growth Quality score breakdown section

Add a section called:

What is driving the score?

Display 4–5 components with weighted contributions such as:

- Retention quality
- Discount reliance
- CAC efficiency
- Contribution quality
- Channel mix quality

Show each as a row with:
- component name
- score or status
- short explanation

Example:
Retention quality: Strong
Discount reliance: Weak
CAC efficiency: Watch
Channel mix quality: Mixed

5. Growth Quality trend chart

Add a section called:

Growth Quality Trend

Show the score trend over the last 6–12 months.

Example:
B+ → B → B- → C+

Add a short explanatory line below the chart.

6. Key growth drivers this month

Add a section called:

Key Growth Drivers This Month

Display 4–6 bullet-point insights such as:

- Repeat purchase rate improved 2.4%
- Discount depth increased 1.8pp
- Meta CAC increased 14%
- Organic mix declined
- Email contribution margin remained strongest

Style consistently with other insight sections.

7. Recommended actions section

Add a section called:

What to do next

Display 3–5 practical actions to improve growth quality.

Example:
- Reduce discount usage on returning customers
- Shift budget away from lowest-margin paid channels
- Strengthen post-purchase email retention flows
- Focus acquisition on products with highest contribution margin

Add small impact tags such as:
High impact
Medium impact
Quick win

Design requirements:

- Keep the page visually consistent with the rest of the app
- Make it feel like a Virtual CFO product rather than a generic marketing dashboard
- Use founder-friendly, commercially oriented language
- Structure all insight blocks so they can later be dynamically generated from real data
