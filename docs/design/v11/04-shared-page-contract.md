# 4 Shared page and evidence contract

## Initial and expanded views

SH01 P1. Default reading order is purpose and context, one verdict, compact cards and one primary next action. Scout has four cards and all diagnostics closed. Profit keeps the Sankey, Cash a compact bridge, Customer Economics a payback curve, Channels one spend/metric trend, and the planner its active working panel. Other details begin closed. Expanded states reveal detail without changing the shared header, figures, evidence status or primary action.

SH02 P1. Explore the Numbers is persistent and discoverable near the top on every page and state. Contextual evidence links may supplement it. Material caveats, unknown status and relevant cash limitations cannot be hidden behind expansion. Show a concise sample label once; never substitute example results automatically when real data are missing.

## States and intelligent silence

SH03 P0. Implement loading, no connection, incomplete, stale, failed, verified quiet, actionable and sample states. Unknown is not zero. Preserve unaffected supported totals and clearly dated prior snapshots. In verified quiet, show the check scope and time without inventing a new alert. A previously acknowledged historical decline can remain in the cards without becoming a new notification.

The Scout quiet example is quiet relative to the last completed review, not a claim that profit improved versus last year. The incomplete example retains sales and dated cash but withholds CM3 and EBITDA because 12 products lack cost mappings. These are alternative fixtures, not simultaneous states of the complete quarterly example.

## Evidence and generated commentary

SH04 P0. Each result carries metric ID, business, currency, timezone, period and comparison, snapshot ID, formula version, source coverage, cost coverage, inclusions/exclusions, denominator, rounding and readiness status. Evidence reproduces the displayed amount. Recommendations additionally carry rationale, affected records, eligible actions, guardrails and the basis of any range.

Deterministic calculations and approved rules precede generated narrative. Narrative cannot invent causal effects, confidence, financial impact or source coverage. Immutable snapshot and scenario versions preserve the evidence behind past decisions. Imports must be idempotent and support late refunds, retries, revoked access and backfills.

## Responsive and accessible behaviour

SH05 P1. At 1440 × 900 and 390px, the verdict and next action must be discoverable. Use one mobile column, labelled navigation, readable charts and accessible values tables. Status uses text/icons as well as colour. Keyboard users operate selectors, sliders and disclosures with focus retained and aria-expanded exposed. Errors identify the field and recovery step. Sticky results must not obscure inputs or duplicate announcements.

## Action records

SH06 P0/P1. Each accepted action has ID, baseline/rule version, owner, target, review date, assumptions, status and history. Accept, adapt, dismiss, snooze and reschedule preserve the reason and original dates where relevant. Completion is not proof of savings. No automatic financial execution follows acceptance.
