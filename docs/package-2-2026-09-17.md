## Delivered

- Growth Quality, Pricing & Discounts, Marketing Efficiency and Cash Control now use shared store-local period selection and verified sales: net/gross product sales, original AOV and discount amounts. Removed legacy order-containing-month lookbacks and Marketing snapshot requests. A shared VerifiedSalesSummary presents adapter values without new financial arithmetic.

No new public endpoint, installed connection or token provider. See [Shopify readiness](shopify-connection-readiness-2026-09-17.md).
