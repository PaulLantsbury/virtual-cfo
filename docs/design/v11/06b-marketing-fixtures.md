# 6B Marketing and cohort fixtures

The current July–September acquisition fixture has £90,000 eligible spend, 2,700 new customers and £26 first-order contribution before acquisition per customer. CAC is £33.3333; the displayed £7.33 deficit totals £19,800 from unrounded inputs. This is an initial contribution gap, not maximum cash funding or a guaranteed amount outstanding for 55 days.

First-order break-even CAC including allocated overheads is £26 − £121,680 / 9,980 = £13.8076, displayed £13.81. It assumes one first order per acquired customer and average overhead per order. It is indicative, not a universal maximum, marginal CAC, lifetime ceiling, cash break-even or whole-business guarantee. Negative results mean no nonnegative CAC breaks even on this basis.

## Separate mature cohort

The April–June cohort is a distinct fictional population of 2,700 customers, observed through 30 September. Its matched CAC is also £33.3333; this coincidence does not make it the current-quarter cohort. Gross profit at 90 days is £75/customer, giving GP:CAC 2.25. Contribution before acquisition is £55, leaving £21.67 after CAC before overheads. The fixture assumes no additional repeat-marketing deduction; production must define this explicitly.

Cumulative contribution at day 0/30/55/60/90 is £26/£30/£33.3333/£38/£55, crossing CAC at day 55. Recovery is gradual. July–September cohorts remain immature for a complete 90-day result. New/returning net product revenue is 40%/60% for the selected quarter; do not invent monthly composition. Refunds and unknown identity require explicit allocation.
